01 · CONSULTING SCOPE

Process discovery and requirements

For teams that need a shared understanding before choosing or changing software.

Typical deliverables

A process map, stakeholder and pain-point summary, prioritized requirements, acceptance criteria and open questions.

What we need to start

A walkthrough with process owners, access to approved sample documents and a named decision-maker.

02 · CONSULTING SCOPE

Implementation planning and readiness

For teams preparing a rollout, migration or go-live review.

Typical deliverables

A milestone plan, responsibility map, risk register, UAT outline, readiness checklist and handover plan.

What we need to start

An agreed scope, accountable technical and business owners, and evidence for the readiness review.

03 · CONSULTING SCOPE

Integration and workflow scoping

For teams planning healthcare integration or an operational workflow.

Typical deliverables

A system-context diagram, data and identifier map, exception scenarios, validation plan and specialist handoff brief.

What we need to start

Approved sample data, system-owner participation and vendor documentation where available.

How an engagement starts

  1. Share the problem, current tools, stakeholders and desired outcome.
  2. Agree the scope, deliverables, responsibilities, timeline and commercial terms.
  3. Review the findings together and decide the next step.

These are examples of possible engagement scopes, not fixed-price packages. Configuration, coding, clinical validation, security approval and production operations require separately agreed responsibilities and qualified specialists where needed.
