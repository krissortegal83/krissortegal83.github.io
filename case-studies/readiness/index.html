My contribution

I shaped this portfolio exercise around a business problem, explicit acceptance rules and reviewable exceptions. The implementation and documentation were developed with AI assistance. It demonstrates an approach; it is not a record of a former employer's delivery.

The approach

The lab distinguishes critical readiness controls from an advisory communication control. A recommendation follows the evidence state rather than an average completion score.

Decisions and tradeoffs

Separate critical and advisory controls

UAT, data, rollback and training are critical; communications is advisory in this example.

Make the recommendation traceable

An incomplete critical control means NO GO. Only advisory work pending means CONDITIONAL GO; all complete means GO.

Reassess when evidence changes

Reopening a critical item blocks the recommendation again.

What this demonstrates

A working example with explicit rules and repeatable checks. No client outcome, cost saving or production performance claim is inferred from this exercise.

Moving toward a real implementation

For a real engagement: tailor the controls and thresholds, assign accountable owners, attach evidence, rehearse rollback and obtain the designated business and technical approvals.

Scope and limits

These thresholds are illustrative. The demo does not authorize a production launch or replace a steering committee, regulatory review or organization-specific acceptance process.
